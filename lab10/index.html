4

Design Reflection

This extension reuses Lab 9’s coordinated GDP choropleth and non-contiguous cartogram. The novel input is one-shot voice recognition for selecting an economy, which then uses the existing selector to highlight that economy in both views. The supported task is finding a named economy and comparing its colour and GDP-scaled silhouette. Voice is particularly useful for geographically small targets such as Singapore or Hong Kong SAR, where precise pointing on a world map can be awkward even though saying the name is straightforward. It is not a replacement for conventional interaction: the selector, mouse, keyboard, Clear selection, and Reset map remain essential fallbacks. Browser support differs, permission can be denied, and background noise, accents, pronunciation, ambiguous names, or a browser’s network-backed recognition service can cause errors. Visible Heard and Action feedback makes those limits inspectable rather than silent.
